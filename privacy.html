Auto Gen Vids

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

1Summary

Auto Gen Vids (“the App”) is a desktop tool that runs on the user’s own computer and publishes the user’s own educational videos to their TikTok and YouTube accounts. The App has no user database and no servers of its own. Account tokens and video files stay on the user’s computer, except when a video is uploaded to the platform the user chose.

2Data the App accesses

  • TikTok: with your permission through TikTok Login Kit, basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) and creator information needed to post (such as available privacy options). Scopes used: user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish.
  • YouTube: with your permission through Google OAuth, your channel name and the ability to upload videos and thumbnails. Scopes used: youtube.upload, youtube.readonly.
  • Content you create: lesson scripts, captions, hashtags, video and thumbnail files you make in the App.

3How the data is used

  • To show which account is connected and to upload the video, caption, hashtags and cover or thumbnail that you choose, only when you start an upload.
  • The App does not read your other videos, messages, followers or analytics, and does not post anything automatically without your action.
  • The App does not sell, rent or share your data, and does not use it for advertising or tracking.

4Storage and security

Access and refresh tokens are stored only in local files on your computer. Video files are sent directly from your computer to TikTok or YouTube over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Keep your computer secure, because anyone with access to it could use the connected accounts through the App.

5Third-party services

Uploaded content is processed by the platforms under their own policies: the TikTok Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy. YouTube features use YouTube API Services, subject to the YouTube Terms of Service. To draft scripts and voices, the App may send lesson text (not your account data) to AI services such as Google Gemini.

6Retention and deletion

  • Tokens are kept until you disconnect the account in the App, which deletes them from your computer.
  • You can also revoke access at any time: in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions, and for Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Videos you published stay on TikTok or YouTube until you delete them there.

7Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

This policy may be updated. The effective date above shows the latest version.
